## ic S50
- Cost-benefit and marginal analysis
- [fld]
- Objective: Maximize [#C00000]**net**[#C00000] benefits [#C00000](= total benefits – total costs) / To maximize net benefits from any activity, marginal analysis [#C00000]is required / >Marginal benefit (MB): [#C00000]additional benefit due to an extra unit of the activity (or an extra unit of a good/service) / >Marginal cost (MC): [#C00000]additional cost due to an extra unit / >>Includes opportunity costs!  / Buy / go for the extra unit if MB > MC / _Important Rule:_ / Net benefits are maximized [#C00000]where **MB = MC**
