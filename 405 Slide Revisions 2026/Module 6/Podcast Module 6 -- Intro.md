## What Module 6 will cover

Module 6 is about what a company does when one price is not good enough.

**The trap in a single price.** Every business that picks one price is stuck
with the same bad trade. Set it high and you make a lot on each sale, but you
turn away everyone who would only have bought at less. Set it low and you sell
to almost everybody, but you hand a windfall to the customers who would happily
have paid far more. One price cannot do both jobs, and the rest of the module is
about the ways out.

**What it takes to get out.** Not every company can. Module 6 starts by working
out what you need: some freedom over your own price, customers who genuinely
differ from one another, and a way to stop people buying cheap from you and
reselling dear to each other. That last one is harder than it sounds, and the
class has a good story about a candy company losing that fight on Amazon.

**Knowing who is in front of you.** This is the heart of the module. There are
three ways to charge different people different amounts for much the same thing
– what the class calls *price discrimination* – and which one is open to you
depends entirely on what you know. You might know each individual customer. You
might only know which broad group someone belongs to. Or you might know nothing
at all, in which case you build a menu of choices and let people sort
themselves, which turns out to be the version most businesses actually use.

**One customer, many units.** Then Module 6 changes the question. Forget that
customers differ from each other, and think instead about one person buying
several units. Do you charge for access and let them take as much as they like?
Charge for access and then a price per use? Or drop the price as the same person
buys more? The class works out which of those fits, and the answer turns on one
thing: what it actually costs you to serve one more.

Along the way there is Netflix, Costco, Zipcar, supermarket coupons, and the
price of the second scoop of ice cream. How each one works out is the reward
for showing up.
