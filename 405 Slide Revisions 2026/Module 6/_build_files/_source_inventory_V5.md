**NOTES:** With MC=0, people buy to where the value is zero. You want to capture the entire area under the demand curve. How much does the consumer pay? For a flat fee of $400, he would be just willing to purchase the bundle of 20 units. The company doubles their contribution and extracts all the surplus. 

Equivalent pricing schemes: A sliding scale discount pricing scheme amounts to the same thing – charge lower and lower amounts for each subsequent unit sold. Here you march the consumer down the demand curve and you charge successively lower prices that allow you to capture all the surplus. The quantity discounts come from the seller’s realization that the buyer has a downward sloping demand curve.
